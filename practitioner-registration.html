Declarations & Consent
Please read and confirm each statement

Safeguarding Declaration

I confirm that I am not currently disqualified from working with children or vulnerable adults, and that I have no pending investigations or convictions that would prevent me from doing so. I agree to notify H.O.M.E Hub immediately if this changes.

Lone Working Declaration

I understand and accept that hiring this space carries specific times when I will be responsible for opening and closing the premises myself, and for working entirely alone in the building — with no other H.O.M.E Hub staff, volunteers or other practitioners present. Where I work alone on site at H.O.M.E Hub CIC, I confirm I will do so within the boundaries of the Lone Working & Key Holding Framework provided to me, and I accept responsibility for my own safety while working alone within those agreed procedures. I understand that H.O.M.E Hub CIC’s responsibility in respect of lone working extends to providing a safe system of work, adequate training and safe premises, and does not extend to risks I choose to accept or to actions taken outside the agreed framework. I know that in an emergency while working alone I can contact James Taylor on 07720 710698 or Mark Cox on 07958 427854, and I will report any incident, near-miss or safety concern arising from lone working to H.O.M.E Hub without delay.

As a self-employed practitioner hiring this space independently, I further understand that my lone working arrangements — including opening and closing the premises and being alone in the building — are my own responsibility to manage safely, and that H.O.M.E Hub’s support in this respect extends to the premises and the agreed Lone Working & Key Holding Framework, not to the supervision or conduct of the service I independently provide.

Self-Employed Independence Declaration

I confirm that I operate as a self-employed practitioner (or through my own registered business), and that in providing my service I act entirely on my own behalf and not as an agent, employee, partner or representative of H.O.M.E Hub CIC. I am responsible for my own tax, insurance, professional conduct, and the safety and quality of the service I provide to my own clients. H.O.M.E Hub CIC’s role under this agreement is limited to the hire of space to me, and H.O.M.E Hub CIC accepts no responsibility or liability for the service I deliver, any claims arising from it, or my relationship with my own clients.

Working with H.O.M.E Hub — the shape of our agreement

Registering means you’ve read your hire offer and accept its terms — the arrangement type, spaces, rates and payment terms. You’ll work within the H.O.M.E Hub Safeguarding Policy, Code of Conduct and H&S guidelines. Gifted and exchange arrangements are offered in the spirit of community and may be reviewed with reasonable notice.
